Research Focus

My research focuses on threat discovery and attack attribution. I recruit doctoral students and professional masters. Applicants with a solid foundation in the following directions are welcome: security, vulnerability mining, reverseing and cracking, network traffic analyse, and machine learning. If you are interested in the above information, please send your resume, transcript, personal statement and other materials to Dr. Liu Feng (liufeng@iie.ac.cn). Security researchers are welcome to apply.

Security

Protocol verification, secure system design and vulnerability analysis.

Vulnerability Mining

Automated discovery of vulnerabilities from code and semantic features.

Traffic Analysis

Network traffic analyse and encrypted traffic classification.

Machine Learning

ML for APT detection, active learning and representation learning.
